SAHASis

"Making life a little easier"

Quoting Swami Vivekananda: "If you want to find God, serve man. To reach Narayana you must serve the Daridra Narayanas - the starving millions of India..."

We at SAHAS, the SAHASis are doing our bit towards providing aid through necessary resources like education, care and support to the needy and underprivileged school kids, provide companionship to lonely elders as well as extend our hand to any specific need of the hour.

Vision

To Provide

A secure, happy, healthy childhood to the country's underprivileged children.

And

An interesting and cheerful existence to lonely seniors.

Also

Extending a hand for any need or during any calamity in the nation without restriction.

Take Part

Any Resident or Non Resident Indian who accepts the constitution of SAHAS can enroll as a member of SAHAS as a life member or yearly member.
